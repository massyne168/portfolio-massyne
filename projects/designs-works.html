THE CONCEPT

Designs Works turns a difficult emotion into a visual persona: a veiled face, restrictive hands and thorn-like ornament create a haunting symbolic portrait.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Vivid coral-red fields, carved black display type and an expressive central figure carry an intense contemporary gothic tone.

03 / ANATOMY OF THE ARTWORK
