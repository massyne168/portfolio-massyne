THE CONCEPT

Nocturne — Dark Veil treats darkness as an aesthetic atmosphere. A crown-like headdress and veiled portrait are framed by symbolic diagrams and coded notes.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Silver-gray oversized lettering, black portrait photography-inspired imagery, fine grid symbols and restrained red accents form a detailed collector-style cover.

03 / ANATOMY OF THE ARTWORK

Look a little closer.

01

Quiet portrait

Soft grayscale contrast and covered features build an inward-looking emotional atmosphere.

02

Archival language

Diagram-like details, labels and measured alignment surround the central character.

03

Restrained chroma

Almost-total monochrome allows tiny crimson elements to sharpen the mood.
