THE CONCEPT

Nocturne — Red Sun imagines a silent wanderer in a ruined world. The masked figure and circular red backdrop evoke mystery, survival and ritual.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Weathered ivory paper, high-contrast black armor, a vermilion disc and monumental condensed lettering give the piece its collectible-print character.

03 / ANATOMY OF THE ARTWORK

Look a little closer.

01

Gothic silhouette

The beaked mask, wide hat and polearm create a distinctive instantly readable subject.

02

Red sun motif

A single bold crimson circle organizes the scene and heightens its sense of otherworldliness.

03

Antique print texture

Distressed lettering and ornamental markings create an aged editorial identity.
